# Case 09 — Samsung 1988 source ledger

**Historical/source record.** Samsung Semiconductor, *MOS Memory Data Book* (1988). KM4164B: 2 ms row-refresh contract; eight initialization cycles after 2 ms with no RAS. KM41256A/KM41257A: 4 ms contract; explicit on-chip CBR refresh-address counter; separate CBR counter-test initialization; eight general initialization cycles after 4 ms with no RAS.
